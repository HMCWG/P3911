The record in order

Proposed Adopted Released TBD

IEEE P3911 numbering starts at Meeting 1, the first meeting held after the project was approved (March 2026), and runs to Meeting 3 in August 2026. The seven earlier records, from June 2024 to December 2025, predate the project and carry no meeting number; their dates, venues, proposals and releases are unchanged.

    02 — How to read this page

    What each entry contains

    Every entry lists the proposals that define it, the adoption result for each proposal and the reference-software version that belongs to that meeting. Proposal numbers (M…) identify working-group documents; the version number is the i3DV release built from the adopted tools. Early records and P3911 meetings follow the same layout; only the heading differs.
