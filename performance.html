Performance

RD curves for six common test sequences and nine i3DV reference-software versions of IEEE P3911. Click a legend entry to toggle a version and hover a point for exact rate and PSNR values. Versions are compared on the curves themselves.

01 — Rate–distortion curves

Choose a sequence

All nine versions are shown by default for every sequence. Two of them reuse the measured points of another version and are drawn dashed with open rings so the original curve stays readable.

The horizontal axis is the coded representation size expressed as rate; the vertical axis is PSNR. Test rate points differ across versions, so compare versions by the overall trend of the curves rather than by single points. 2.1 reuses the 2.0 results, and for VRU_gz, VRU_dg4, Show_Groups_4K and cook_spinach the 8.0 results reuse 7.0.
